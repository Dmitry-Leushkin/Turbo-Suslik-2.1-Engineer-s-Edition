# Instead of a Preface - Who This Book Is For

[Book contents](../README.md) | [Part contents](README.md)

**If** you have begun to understand that you yourself are the source of a significant part of your problems in life - your complexes, resentments, traumas, fears, beliefs, habitual reactions, and all the accumulated weight of the past;

**If** you are genuinely tired of living under negative conditioning, low self-esteem, and chronic insecurity;

**If** you see clearly that, in practical terms, there is no alternative but to throw all this baggage out;

**If** words like “awareness” and “freedom from automatic patterns” are not decorative abstractions for you, but describe a real need - to stop living on autopilot;

**If** you are fed up with techniques that require you to believe in something, imagine something, “feel correctly,” think prescribed thoughts, or manufacture a special state - and you have been looking for a practical tool that requires neither rituals nor self-deception;

**If** you want concrete changes: less mental clutter, less internal conflict, fewer imposed labels, fears, complexes, traumas, and fewer endless replays of the past;

**If** you want to start living in reality instead of the perpetual “later” - once I finish university, get the position, complete the project, find investors, have children, retire, then I will finally start living properly;

**If** you are tired of living in your head, consuming mountains of literature, and attending endless webinars, courses, and training programs supplied by an industry that too often sells empty boxes in attractive packaging;

**If** you are ready to work and understand that there are no shortcuts and never will be;

**If** you are ready to face your inner conflicts, fears, traumas, and uncomfortable truths, despite the pain and discomfort that may arise in the process;

**If** you understand that no one can give you freedom “as a gift” - at best, someone can provoke you, shake you, and confront you with a choice: act or continue talking;

And if, at the same time, you have no desire to spend long years on slow practices and endless conversations - then the techniques described in this book may be of interest to you.

**There is one main obstacle: the refusal to take responsibility for what is now running inside you.** You may not have chosen what happened to you. You may not have installed the original damage. That changes nothing about who has to deal with it now. If you believe that whoever caused the damage must remain responsible for repairing it; if you expect someone to “fix” your life for you, hand you a magic pill, guide you step by step, or “heal” you; if you are not prepared to persistently dismantle the mental clutter accumulated over the years - close this book. It is not for you.

**In short, this book is for people who want to change, are ready to change, and will actually do the work.** For those who will work instead of complaining, nursing resentments, or endlessly outsourcing responsibility to their past, the people close to them, “circumstances,” fate, or “bad techniques that don’t work.” This is a book for practitioners.

For ordinary people - men and women, young and old, grandfathers and grandmothers (better late than never) - who have decided to take charge of their own psyche and begin putting it in order, without fairy tales and without self-deception.

**This system is a tool for work.** More importantly, it is a tool for **independent work**. We are talking about individual practice without teachers, mentors, trainers, coaches, seminars, webinars, chats, Zoom calls, or any other form of built-in “social support.”

At the very beginning of Turbo-Suslik, back in 2008, I was convinced that a person who genuinely wants to bring order to their own mind, and is ready to do the work, is capable of accomplishing a great deal independently. More than eighteen years have passed since then, and the experience of tens of thousands of users has confirmed this repeatedly.

You really can produce substantial changes completely on your own through relatively simple but systematic procedures, described in precise detail below.

I do not promise easy solutions. Changes can happen relatively quickly, and often do. But there will be no shortcuts. You will have to work, and speed also has its price. The ways in which resistance and system “pushback” typically appear will be discussed separately in the section on “pendulums.” If you are ready for real work and possible difficulties - including intense discomfort, emotional pain, and sobering disappointments - continue reading.

I do not claim that this system is “the most effective” or “the best.” There is no universally best technique outside the context of a particular person, task, and starting condition. This is simply one tool. For a certain category of people who are ready for this kind of work, it may prove extremely effective. If you belong to that category and get results, I will be sincerely glad. If you do not, nothing is wrong. You need a different tool. Go and find it.

One more important point: Turbo-Suslik is not designed as a spot repair for one or two isolated problems. It is aimed at a cumulative, system-level effect, produced through regular work with large volumes of internal material and automatic patterns. If you only want to “fix a couple of glitches,” this is not the best tool.

And once again: this book is for those who are ready to become 100% their own engineer, mechanic, and test subject. There are no teachers here, no rescuers, and no “hotline” you can call to have everything fixed for you. There is you, your problem, and the tool.

That is all.
