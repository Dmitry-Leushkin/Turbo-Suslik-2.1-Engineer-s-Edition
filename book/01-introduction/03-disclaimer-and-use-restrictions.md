Unofficial part:

IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION:

Now for the unofficial version.

Turbo-Suslik is a tool for maintenance work on your own psyche. A screwdriver or a wrench. It does not hold your hand, decide what is safe for you, or take responsibility for what you do with it.

You pick it up and use it at your own risk, on your own, with full responsibility for the consequences. There is no operator sitting at the other end, no supervisor watching your progress, and no support desk waiting to take over when things become uncomfortable.

If you encounter a **pendulum** - a temporary increase in emotional intensity, instability, or system pushback during processing - do not treat it as a malfunction or a reason to quit. Pendulums are a normal part of accelerated change. When old patterns begin to lose stability, connected material may become temporarily more active before the system settles into a new configuration. This is part of the price you pay for speed and real change. Observe what is happening, process the material that surfaces, and continue according to the instructions.

A pendulum, however, is not a convenient label for absolutely any condition. The boundary is not discomfort, pain, or emotional intensity. The boundary is the loss of your ability to observe what is happening, make responsible decisions, function safely, or remain in control of your actions. If that boundary is crossed, stop and contact an appropriately licensed psychiatrist or clinical psychologist. Not a coach, facilitator, motivational expert, or internet shaman. **I do not provide individual advice, guidance, support, or supervision, and I cannot take responsibility for what you choose to do to yourself.**

This book is a technical manual. Nothing more. If you expect care, supervision, rescue, or someone to lead you by the hand, close it now.

\*\*\*

Everything written in this book reflects my personal practical experience using these techniques. I do not claim to present the “ultimate truth,” and I do not claim that my perspective is the only correct one. I describe what I have done myself, what I have observed in my own work, and what I have observed in people who used these methods and shared their results with me.

I may be wrong about some details. Some things may eventually be explained better, implemented differently, or made more effective. That is not critical for one simple reason: I, along with thousands of people who have used the system, have observed concrete practical changes in reactions, behavior, and the overall quality of life. That is enough to regard this approach as workable for a certain group of people.

There is no such thing as a “mandatory result.” You may experience substantial changes, as many people have. You may experience little. You may experience nothing useful at all. No guarantee is possible because too much depends on your starting condition, the amount of work you actually do, your level of honesty, and your willingness to stop lying to yourself.

The restrictions in the formal notice above are not decorative legal wallpaper. If any of them apply to you, stop there. Do not turn recklessness into courage, and do not call ignoring an explicit restriction “taking responsibility.” It is nothing of the kind.

**The process may involve extremely strong negative emotional reactions.** Old material may surface: fear, anger, shame, anxiety, guilt, helplessness - sometimes with considerable intensity. This can be part of the unpacking process. It is not automatically a malfunction, but it is not automatic proof of progress either. The intensity may be considerable; that alone does not mean that something has gone wrong. Pendulums are part of the process, and speed has its price. The relevant boundary is whether you remain capable of observing the process, making responsible decisions, and acting safely. Do not confuse severe discomfort with danger - but do not rename an actual loss of control “deep processing” either.  The system awards no medals for psychological self-demolition. If you are not prepared to face yourself without anesthesia, it is better not to begin.

Over time, many people notice that old illusions and beliefs lose their grip and the emotional background changes: fewer dramas, fewer emotional swings, more calm clarity and detachment. Some people like this. Others hate it, because familiar internal melodrama, victim identity, and social games begin to disappear. **If your traumas are central to your identity, your grievances serve as your capital, and your “complexity” has become a lifestyle, this system is not for you.**

Old motivations and goals may also begin to feel strange, and some social roles may lose their meaning. This can be uncomfortable. If you are strongly attached to status, approval, and the perceived importance of things, it may be better not to begin. This is not about rejecting money, ambition, or material life. It is about no longer selling yourself for validation.

Some users report changes in their physical well-being. Fine. The psyche and the body are not sealed compartments. But an incidental observation is not the purpose of the system and does not turn it into treatment. Do not use someone else’s anecdote as an excuse to smuggle medical expectations into Turbo-Suslik through the back door.

The system is strictly self-directed. If you are looking for a guru, mentor, facilitator, or someone to guide you by the hand, this is not for you. No one is coming to save you here. Either you take full ownership of the work or you close the book.

The restrictions are literal. Under eighteen means under eighteen, not “mature for your age.” During pregnancy or breastfeeding, leave the practice alone. Optional experiments involving intense emotional load are a bad trade during that period.

In short: you use the tool yourself. The risk is yours. The work is yours. The results are yours. The consequences are yours.

If you do not accept that arrangement, close the book and do not continue.
