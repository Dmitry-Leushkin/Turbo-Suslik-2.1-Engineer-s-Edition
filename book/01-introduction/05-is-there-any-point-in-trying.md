# Is There Any Point in “Trying”?

[Book contents](../README.md) | [Part contents](README.md)

It is very easy to determine whether this method is suitable for you. Try it. Consider it a demo version.

After approximately two or three weeks of practice, you should already have enough information to identify the first changes from working with Turbo-Suslik - or their absence.

In other words, even such a short period is enough to form a preliminary impression. This does not mean that your life will be completely transformed in two weeks. It means that by this point you should be able to notice whether anything small but clear and observable has begun to change. A few days after processing an emotionally charged episode, you may find that your perception of it has shifted. A person you used to strongly dislike may no longer trigger the same reaction. A pressing current problem may still be there but clearly feel less intense. A long-standing issue that has been looping through your head for years may suddenly feel different. And so on.

The important thing is not to invent results, persuade yourself that something has happened, or attempt to “believe” or “not believe” in Turbo-Suslik. That is a separate problem altogether. Remain as impartial an observer as you can.

A useful tool should allow itself to be tested. You now have an unusual tool in your hands. Does it work? Test it. Practice. Experiment. But do not become a believer. Stay grounded and keep watching what actually happens. Be open enough to run the test and skeptical enough to observe it honestly. Start from where you are, follow the instructions, and after a couple of weeks compare your actual reactions with what they were before.

If, after four weeks of practice, you can detect no changes at all, pause. Do not push harder merely because you want the experiment to succeed. Continue observing your reactions and general state for another two to four weeks, because some changes may become visible only after active work has stopped. If you still notice nothing, then Turbo-Suslik is most likely not suitable for you.
