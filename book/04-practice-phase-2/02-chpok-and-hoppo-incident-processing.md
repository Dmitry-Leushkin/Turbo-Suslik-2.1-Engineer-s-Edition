# “Chpok” and “Hoppo.” Incident Processing

[Book contents](../README.md) | [Part contents](README.md)

By this point, Phase 1 should be fully complete. Before starting active work on the past, go register on the site (link at the end of the book) and grab two free processors: **Chpok** and **Hoppo** - free registration required.

### The "Chpok" Processor

**Chpok** is the simplest, fastest processor in the whole system. Narrow job: strip the emotional charge off specified mental material.

"Charge," here, means a bundled payload of:

- visual imagery,
- emotion,
- somatic sensation,
- thought,

all of which auto-fire together whenever attention references a given episode, person, or state.

**Chpok** runs fast precisely because it skips deep analysis entirely. It doesn't touch root decisions, beliefs, personality aspects, or downstream constructs built on top of the charge. It just strips the charge off the cached object - a cache eviction, not a recompute - and does it efficiently.

This processor is optimized for speed and simplicity. Not depth.

### The "Hoppo" Processor

**Hoppo** is a specialized handler scoped to forgiveness. Function: automatic removal of grievance, resentment, and internal conflict - toward yourself and toward others.

Built on Ho'oponopono principles, heavily simplified and adapted for this runtime. Inside TS, Hoppo gets invoked:

- when processing people tied to past episodes;
- inside almost every advanced-tier automated protocol.

Both processors install the standard way: read the protocol once, aloud or silently - that's the full registration step. Once read, the processor's considered installed and ready to call. Invocation: point attention at the material, repeat the keyword until a yawn fires.

### What Processing Episodes Looks Like in Practice

Pull the first episode off the list, bring it into attention (no immersion, no re-living), start processing.

Two primary options:

1. **Run** **`Chpok`** - the charge clears fast, often within minutes to hours. Shallow eviction, quick turnaround.
1. **Run** **`Process this`** - deeper processing, but integration of the resulting change can take longer to fully converge.

You can chain both: fast charge-strip via `Chpok` first, then a deeper pass with `Process this` on the same episode later.

### Example

Episode: `"English teacher insulted me in class, 7th grade."`

Point attention at it and loop:

`The teacher insulted me - Chpok`

`The teacher insulted me - Chpok`

`...`

Until a yawn fires, or up to 10 iterations.

No yawn? Leave it, move on - the subconscious has already queued it for processing regardless.

An hour or more later, check back on the episode:

- reads as neutral and boring now → charge is cleared;
- charge weaker but not fully gone → queue it for another pass later;
- charge unchanged → in most cases, that's a resistance flag, not a tool failure.

### Working with Resistance

If the charge won't clear, log every thought and feeling that surfaces around the episode and the act of processing it. E.g.:

- `"I don't want to let this go"`
- `"They hurt me"`
- `"I hate them"`
- `"This technique doesn't work"`
- `"This won't help me"`

Run each line separately through `Process this`, to yawn or up to 10 reps.

Then re-run `Chpok` on the original episode.

### Working with People

Once every episode tied to a specific person is cleared, process that person's overall image as its own object:

`English teacher - Chpok`

`...`

To yawn or up to 10 reps. Then:

`English teacher - Hoppo`

`...`

To yawn or up to 10 reps. Then the final step - neutralize the link itself:

`English teacher and Me - Merge`

`...`

To yawn or up to 10 reps. That closes out processing at the episode level, the personality level, and the relationship level, in sequence.

If a fresh episode with this person surfaces later, just process it separately. Reprocessing is fully allowed - not a sign anything failed the first time.

### Important Clarification

Clearing the charge off episodes and people doesn't guarantee every uncomfortable feeling tied to the situation vanishes.

Reason's simple: most of the ongoing discomfort often gets generated *after* the original event - thoughts, interpretations, conclusions layered on top. That's secondary material, and it needs its own separate pass, via `Chpok` or `Process this`.

This is a normal working state, not a "the tool doesn't work" state.

### Universality of "Chpok"

`Chpok` isn't scoped only to past episodes. It also applies to:

- current emotional states,
- bodily sensations,
- somatic symptoms (without replacing actual medical care).

The invocation pattern's constant across all of them: flag the material with attention, repeat the keyword. For deep, multi-layer work, `Process this` is the better call. For a fast charge-strip, `Chpok`.

### Automatic Processing

Lower-priority episodes can get batched into automated protocols and run overnight, no manual supervision. The batch job:

- strips charge,
- processes secondary material,
- initiates forgiveness,
- runs circular processing,
- neutralizes linkages.

Slower to converge than a manual pass, but considerably lower-effort - same eventual-consistency tradeoff covered earlier: throughput and convenience against immediacy. Which one you reach for is a matter of stage and preference, not correctness.
