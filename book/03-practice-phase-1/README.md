# Practice - Phase 1

[Book contents](../README.md)

Phase 1 is scoped for roughly one month of independent work. Its purpose: lay a stable base for everything faster and automated that comes after it. Over this phase, you establish a stable baseline for reducing subjective division of the world into categories, reducing duality and evaluative perception, increasing emotional balance, and continuing the ongoing work with the past.

Phase 1 runs on three tracks. All three are mandatory - none are optional, all need to ship before you move forward:

1. **Polarity processing** - neutralizing polarities from the provided list, plus your own personal set.
1. **Emotional scale calibration** - starting the process toward emotional balance and neutrality.
1. **Basic past clearing** - running your first automated processing routine against historical material.

Optionally, if your system can handle the extra load, you can start gradually processing some of your existing problems manually - purely to build hands-on experience and practical skill. This step isn't required in Phase 1 and can be skipped entirely if you don't want to go deeper into specific problems yet.
