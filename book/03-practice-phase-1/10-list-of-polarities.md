# List of Polarities

[Book contents](../README.md) | [Part contents](README.md)

Below is a large compiled list of polarities for neutralization (about 430 items). The neutralization procedure is simple - to remind you, you say the first polarity, then the second polarity, then the word **“Merge,”** and repeat this until yawning occurs. For example:<br>
<br>
Love - hate - Merge<br>
Love - hate - Merge<br>
Love - hate - Merge<br>
Love - hate - Merge<br>
Love - hate - Merge

Yawn fires here - job's confirmed started, move to the next polarity in the queue.

If 10 iterations run with no yawn, move on anyway - the subconscious will neutralize this pair over time regardless of whether you got a visible confirmation signal. When calling the polarities (again - no need to say them aloud), briefly try to feel each pole, at least on the first pass. Not a hard requirement though - don't get stuck trying to force it.

**No re-runs on already-processed polarities.** Once you've initiated processing on a given pair via the procedure above, cross it off the list and don't come back to it. Re-triggering neutralization on the same pair multiple times doesn't speed anything up - it's a wasted call that just adds unnecessary load. To be direct about it: you fire the job once per polarity, then stop thinking about it entirely - it resolves in the background over time, no supervision needed.

**Rate-limit yourself early.** Don't take on more than 10–15 polarities a day at the start. As the system adapts, throughput can scale up significantly, but always watch the body's actual response and don't overprocess. If your system is signaling it's done for the day, it's done - that's not a suggestion, treat it as a hard limit. Volume and pacing get full coverage in the "Work Plan" chapter.

If a given polarity reads as complete nonsense to you - doesn't parse, doesn't mean anything - skip it. Not every entry needs to land.

The list is sorted alphabetically. Full run-through, worst case, clears inside a month without strain.
