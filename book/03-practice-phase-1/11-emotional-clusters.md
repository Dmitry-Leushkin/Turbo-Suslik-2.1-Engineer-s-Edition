# Emotional Clusters

[Book contents](../README.md) | [Part contents](README.md)

**Repeating this once more since it matters.** Processing the emotional states in these clusters is part of Phase 1. Refresher on the procedure: pull an emotional state from the list, say its name (aloud or silently), while attempting to at least minimally register the actual felt state, then call `Process this`, loop until a yawn fires, then move to the next state.

Example - processing `Depressed`. Say (aloud or internally) `"Depressed"`, briefly registering the state, then `"Process this"`. Loop to yawn:

`Depressed - process this`<br>
`Depressed - process this`<br>
`Depressed - process this`<br>
`Depressed - process this`<br>
`Depressed - process this`

Yawn fires here - move to the next state, or resume whatever else you were doing. If 10 iterations run with no yawn, move on regardless - the material processes either way, confirmed signal or not.

**No re-runs on already-processed states.** Once you've initiated processing on a given state, cross it off and don't come back to it. Running the same state's job multiple times doesn't speed anything up - it just costs you a headache and adds unnecessary load for zero gain. To be blunt about it: fire the call once per state, then stop thinking about it - it resolves in the background over time.

**Rate-limit at the start.** Don't take on more than 10–15 states at the very beginning. As the system adapts, throughput can scale up - but keep watching your own reaction and your body's actual response as the ground truth here, not your ambition.

The state list is built on **Lester Levenson's Sedona Method** emotional scale (USA). Start from the lowest-tone state (`Apathy`) and work upward toward higher-tone states as you progress.
